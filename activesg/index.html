Frequently asked questions

What should I know about the new pool gantries?
  • We're progressively reintroducing gantries at all ActiveSG pools for entry and exit in the long term.
  • Scan in the way you did before, and scan out with the same method.
  • Find pools with gantries at go.gov.sg/activesg-gantries.
Will gym entry and exit be changing?
  • We're introducing gantries across all ActiveSG pools first.
  • Entry for gyms will remain the same for now, but we'll post updates when it changes.
How do I swim at the arena pool used for the World Aquatics Championships (WCH)?
  • From 31 Oct to 31 Dec 2025, individuals aged 16 and above can enter with a feature pool pass.
  • The new 3-metre competition pool was used for the World Aquatics Championships.
What are SG60 ActiveSG credits, and when do they expire?

Credits can be used on eligible ActiveSG services, and expiry details will be shown in your wallet and service notices.

Beware of government impersonation scams

All official government SMS will show “gov.sg” as the sender. You will never be asked for money or your details over the phone. If they do, hang up and call ScamShield at 1799.
